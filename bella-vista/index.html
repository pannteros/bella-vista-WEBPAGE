Our Story

A Family Tradition
Since 1987

Bella Vista was born in the heart of Athens when Gianni Rossi, a young chef from Naples, decided to bring the true taste of Neapolitan pizza to Greece. With nothing more than a wood-fired oven, a family recipe, and an unshakable belief in honest ingredients, he opened our doors on a quiet evening in October 1987.

Today, three generations later, we still use the same dough recipe — slow-fermented for 48 hours — and the same oven that Gianni built with his own hands. Every pizza that leaves our kitchen carries that original philosophy: simplicity, quality, and soul.
